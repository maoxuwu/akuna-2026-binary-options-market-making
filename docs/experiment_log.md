# Supplementary prototype — experiment log

[Supplementary research](supplementary_research.md) ·
[Submitted-strategy experiments](experiment_log_line_b.md)

> Competition experiments, condensed for the research archive. Scores, version outcomes and subsequent corrections are retained. Historical working labels are qualified below; see [§14](results_and_postmortem.md) for the retrospective.

The experiments that touched the real evaluation, essentially one line
each (quick confirmatory re-runs are folded into their parent rows).
The prototype builds are lower-case (v1…v13); the submitted strategy's
separately numbered versions (V1…V47) live in experiment_log_line_b.md. Scores are
out of 16 scored sessions (deterministic; per-session score ≈ a linear
function of P&L rank). Sessions are referred to by their position in the
scored list (C5–C20). "Reject" always means *reverted and archived*, never
silently dropped; the mechanism-level reason is the point of this file.

Conventions: SQ = stub quoter (0.01/0.99), FW(w) = fixed-width quoter,
markout = next-day theo move signed by trade direction, knife-edge = a
session won by a margin under ~$3.

> Post-hoc note on vocabulary (added after the results): "closed",
> "information wall", "information-theoretic floor" and "censoring-correct"
> were the campaign's working labels and are kept below as written. They
> mean "no variant tested on the visible set improved this, and the cause
> identified is mechanism-level" — verdicts over the finite experiments
> listed, not proofs over all policies. Several of these walls were later
> exceeded by another execution configuration (writeup [§9](line_b_and_submission.md)), which is the
> clearest reminder of that distinction.

## Experiment-selection conventions

1. **Per-session verdicts, never totals.** Every experiment is judged on all
   16 sessions and their margins; the min-margin (maximin) rule vetoes
   average-improving changes that thin the weakest win.
2. **Dual gates after the overfitting audit:** visible ≥ 13.8 AND no
   detectable paired-seed degradation (no regime significantly negative,
   no catastrophic tail; the lab seeds are a development set, not a
   holdout). One oracle is never enough once you've optimized against it
   fifteen times.
3. **Mechanism-level causes of death.** A rejected idea is only closed when
   the *reason* is structural (censoring, information wall, shared artery) —
   these diagnoses are hypotheses about the tested configurations, not proofs that further variants cannot work.
4. **Bit-identity as evidence.** "No-op on the visible set" is a checkable
   claim (per-session P&L to the cent), used to ship safety layers (E27,
   E39, E40) without an observed regression on those inputs.
